About Us

Our Legacy & Heritage

Aravinda High School was founded with a vision to provide truly holistic CBSE education that nurtures every dimension of a child's personality. Nestled in the serene surroundings of Kunchanapalli, Guntur District, we have grown into one of the most trusted educational institutions in the region.

1500+Students

From the Principal's Desk

"At Aravinda High School, we believe education goes far beyond textbooks. Our commitment to deep holistic education ensures that every child develops intellectually, emotionally, and physically." — Dr. Lakshmi Devi, Principal

Affiliated to the CBSE (Affiliation No. 130633), our campus features smart classrooms, state-of-the-art laboratories, a vast library with over 15,000 books, and excellent sports facilities spread across lush green grounds.

Guiding Principles & Values

Vision

To nurture future leaders with knowledge, integrity, and compassion who will contribute meaningfully to a global society while being rooted in our cultural heritage.

Mission

Provide deep holistic education combining academic excellence with character development, empowering students to discover their true potential.

Integrity

Academics

Curriculum & Departments

We offer the CBSE curriculum from Class VI through Class XII with Science, Commerce, and Humanities streams at the senior secondary level.

Academic Departments:

  • Science — Physics, Chemistry, Biology
  • Mathematics
  • Languages — English, Hindi, Sanskrit
  • Social Studies — History, Geography, Civics
  • Computer Science & IT
  • Fine Arts & Creative Expression
